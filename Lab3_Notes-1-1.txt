        		4-1) shift to the 'left' : (logical or arithmetic shift to the left)

					4-1-1) logical shift to the 'left'
					4-1-2) arithmetic shift to the 'left'

				* Note: logical shift to the 'left' = arithmetic shift to the 'left'



			example for logical / arithmetic shift to the left:

					a= 23 (decimal) : 010111 (binary) ---------> shift left by '1' bit : 101110 (binary) : 46 (decimal)

					a= 23 (decimal) : 010111 (binary) ---------> shift left by '2' bit : 011100 (binary) : 69 (decimal)

							......

				* Note: shifting a number to the 'left' by (n-bits) = multiplication that number by (2^n)
